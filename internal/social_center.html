Platform Strategies
LinkedIn · B2B Authority

The "Founder's Desk" Series

Facility managers and property CEOs are on LinkedIn. Instead of selling cleaning, Zan sells compliance and risk management. Position OTIS as the authority on Quebec labor law and commercial hygiene standards.

Post: Break down the CPEEP /hr parity decree — what "Joint Liability" means for building owners who hire non-compliant subcontractors.
Post: The true hidden cost of cheap night cleaners — security breaches, unlocked doors, insurance policy voids.
Post: "5 things to check on your cleaning contract before it auto-renews." — drives DM inquiries.
Format: Long-form text posts with 1 striking stat, no image, CTA to download the OTIS Compliance PDF.
📊 Target Audience
Property Managers · Facility Directors · COOs · Office Managers · HR Directors · Montreal + Laval + South Shore · 35–60 age range
3x/week Tue/Thu/Sat best days 7–9am EST best time
Instagram Reels · Visual Brand

Office managers are sick of complaints — empty soap dispensers, dirty microwaves, missed shifts. Target their exact pain points with direct-response infographics that sell the problem before the solution.

Ad 1 — Split Card: "Your current cleaner" (missing shifts, no GPS, no insurance) vs "The OTIS Standard" (GPS clock-in, ISSA-certified, insured up to $2M).
Ad 2 — Offer: "Free 1-Month Restroom Supply Restock with any 12-month contract." Limited to 5 businesses/month.

Google Business · Local SEO

The Review Acceleration Engine

Google Maps is Montreal's #1 cleaning company discovery platform. A business with 50+ reviews and 4.8★ wins the click every time — even against bigger competitors with lower prices.

After every job: Send automated SMS/email → "Rate your experience → [Google link]". 1 click, 20-second review.
Post a Google Business Update 2x/week — before/after photo with keyword-rich caption ("post-construction cleaning Plateau Montreal").
Enable Google Q&A — pre-answer common objections ("Do you bring your own supplies?" "Are you insured?").
Target keywords: "commercial cleaning Montreal", "office cleaning Laval", "post-construction cleaning NDG".
🎯 KPI Targets
50+ reviews in 90 days · 4.8★ minimum rating · Top-3 Maps pack for 5 target keywords · <2hr response time on all reviews
TikTok · Virality Play

The "Overnight Crew" Documentary

TikTok rewards authenticity over production quality. A raw, handheld POV of the OTIS night crew silently transforming a corporate office at 2AM has massive viral potential — particularly for reaching property managers aged 28–45.

Series: "We clean this Montreal office every night for $0.08/sqft. Here's what 2AM looks like." — behind-the-scenes crew POV.
Hook formula: Start every video mid-action (scrubber already running, not intro). First 0.5 seconds determines TikTok distribution.
Trend-jack: Post before/after videos during popular cleaning-content hashtag cycles (#CleaningTok, #SatisfyingClean).
CTA: "DM us 'OFFICE' for a free walkthrough estimate." Converts comments to pipeline without a website visit.
📊 Strategy
Organic reach first — paid TikTok boost only on videos that hit 5K+ organic views. Hashtags: #SmallBusiness #OfficeLife #CleanTok #Montreal
Email · Lead Nurture

The 5-Touch Follow-Up Sequence

Most B2B cleaning leads require 5–8 touchpoints before signing. Automate the follow-up so no lead goes cold after the first call or site visit.

Email 1 (Day 0): "Thank you for your inquiry — here's what to expect from OTIS." Attach the Service Overview PDF.
Email 2 (Day 2): "The 3 questions to ask any cleaning company before signing." OTIS answers all 3.
Email 3 (Day 5): Social proof blast — 3 client testimonials + a before/after from a similar industry vertical.
Email 4 (Day 9): "Your free site walkthrough is still available — we have 2 slots left this week." Scarcity trigger.
Email 5 (Day 14): Breakup email — "No worries if timing isn't right. Here's a direct line to Zan: 438-935-9725."
🔧 Tools
Mailchimp (free tier) · Brevo · or embedded in the OTIS CRM. Trigger: New quote form submission auto-enrols lead into the sequence.
