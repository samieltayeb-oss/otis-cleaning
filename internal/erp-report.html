MODULE 4: QUEBEC LABOR & CPEEP PARITY DECREE COMPLIANCE

Enforced by Émile (Quebec Legal Sentinel). Commercial cleaning in Greater Montreal is legally governed by the Decree respecting building service employees in the Montreal region (CPEEP). Non-compliant contractors expose themselves and their clients to severe joint liability. OTIS uses decree compliance as an offensive sales weapon.

Mandatory Legal Labor Cost Economics (CPEEP March 2026 Statutory Schedule):
  • Class B Base Cleaner Wage: 23 / hr (Mandatory statutory floor).
  • Statutory Burden (34.0%): CNESST worker safety insurance (4.8%), QPP/RRQ (5.95%), Employment Insurance (2.28%), QPIP (0.69%), Statutory Holiday & Vacation Pay (8.0%), CPEEP Parity Committee Levy (0.5%), Health Services Fund (4.26%), Uniform/Safety Allowance (1.52%).
  • Real Hourly Labor Break-Even Cost: $31.15 / hr per operative.
  • OTIS Commercial Billing Floor: $38.00 – $48.00 / hr (Guarantees 38%–45% operating gross margin).
Regulatory Body Statutory Requirement Risk of Non-Compliance OTIS Compliance Weapon
CPEEP Parity Committee Mandatory minimum wages (23/h) and monthly employer levies. Civil wage claims; retroactive assessments against the building owner. Attestation de conformité CPEEP issued with every commercial proposal.
CNESST Worker safety coverage, hazardous materials (WHMIS/SIMDUT) training. Workplace stop orders, massive fines, personal director liability. Valid CNESST Clearance Certificate and WHMIS-certified operatives.
Civil Code Article 14 Building owners and property managers have joint and several liability (responsabilité solidaire). Clients forced to pay back-wages if cleaning contractor goes bankrupt or evades decree. "The Article 14 Shield": Proves OTIS protects the property manager from joint liability.
