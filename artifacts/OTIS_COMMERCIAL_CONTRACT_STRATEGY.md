## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Historically, OTIS has delivered recurring commercial cleaning services on an informal, month-to-month basis without fixed-term agreements. While this zero-friction approach helped acquire initial clients, it leaves OTIS with **severe revenue vulnerability**: any client can cancel on 24 hours' notice without cause, disrupting labor schedules, destroying cash flow predictability, and exposing OTIS to unrecoverable equipment and onboarding investments.

### The Strategic Purpose of OTIS Commercial Agreements
The objective is **not to trap customers** or construct punitive legal cages. In the commercial janitorial sector, punitive contracts generate adversarial relationships, poor cooperation, and destructive online reviews.

The objective is to establish:
1. **Predictable Cash Flow:** Secure a committed baseline of Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) to finance payroll and equipment.
2. **Operational Stability:** Accurate labor scheduling and route density planning under the Quebec CPEEP Decree.
3. **Mutual Accountability:** Clearly defined Scopes of Work (SOW), cleaning checklists, and agreed performance standards.
4. **Fair Commercial Commitment:** A two-way street where OTIS commits guaranteed staffing, machinery, and price locks, while the customer commits to a defined operating term.
5. **Investment Protection:** Legitimate recovery of upfront facility onboarding costs, initial deep-clean subsidies, and promotional discounts if a client exits prematurely without cause.

## 3. QUEBEC LEGAL FRAMEWORK FOR COMMERCIAL SERVICE CONTRACTS

Commercial cleaning agreements in Quebec are classified as **Contracts of Enterprise or for Services** (*Contrats d'entreprise ou de service*), governed by Articles 2098 to 2129 of the *Civil Code of Québec* (CCQ).

### 1. Unilateral Resiliation by the Client (Article 2125 CCQ)
* **The Statutory Rule:** Article 2125 CCQ states:  
  > *"The client may unilaterally resiliate the contract at any time, even if the work has already been begun."*
* **B2B vs. Consumer Application:** In consumer contracts, this right is strictly protected. However, established Quebec jurisprudence (*Pelletier c. Ruest*, *D. & G. Lingerie*, and Quebec Court of Appeal precedents) confirms that in **commercial (B2B) contracts between sophisticated business parties**, the parties may contractually modify, circumscribe, or waive the right to unilateral termination under Article 2125 CCQ, provided the waiver clause is clear, express, and unambiguous.

### 2. Consequences of Resiliation (Article 2129 CCQ)
* If resiliation occurs, Article 2129 CCQ stipulates that the client must pay the contractor:
  1. The agreed price for work already performed up to the date of resiliation.
  2. Actual costs and documented expenses incurred by the contractor in preparation for the service.
  3. The value of supplies or property that cannot be returned or repurposed.
  4. Any direct injury or damages suffered by the contractor, where justified.

### 3. Penal Clauses and Liquidated Damages (Articles 1622–1623 CCQ)
* **The Legal Trap:** Inexperienced cleaning companies frequently write clauses stating: *"If the client terminates early, they must immediately pay 100% of all remaining months of the contract term."*
* **The Judicial Reality:** Quebec courts consistently strike down or drastically reduce such clauses under **Article 1623 CCQ** as an **abusive penal clause** (*clause pénale abusive*). A contractor cannot claim 100% of future gross revenue because they are no longer incurring labor wages, payroll taxes, or chemical expenses for unperformed cleans.
* **The Defensible OTIS Solution:** To ensure complete legal enforceability, OTIS's early termination fee (ETF) is structured as a **fair pre-estimate of unrecovered onboarding investments, promotional benefit clawbacks, and reasonable notice periods**, rather than an unenforceable windfall penalty.

## 5. CONTRACT OPTION A: 6-MONTH COMMERCIAL SERVICE AGREEMENT

* **Target Account:** First-time commercial clients, skeptical facility managers, smaller offices, or companies transitioning away from unsatisfactory previous cleaners.
* **Term:** Fixed six (6) calendar months from the Commencement Date.
* **Pricing Standard:** Full standard commercial rate (e.g., $44.00–$48.00/hr or $0.15–$0.25/sq ft/mo).
* **Payment Terms:** Net 15 days via pre-authorized debit (PAD), EFT, or corporate credit card. Invoiced on the 1st of each service month.
* **Scope of Work (SOW):** Exhaustive checklist defining Daily, Weekly, and Monthly tasks with designated square footage.
* **Quality Assurance Gate:** Includes a **Day-30 Joint Performance Review**. If OTIS fails to meet agreed SLAs during the first 30 days after receiving written notice to cure, the client may exit without penalty.
* **Renewal:** Automatically converts to month-to-month service upon expiration unless renewed for a new term or terminated with 30 days' written notice.

## 6. CONTRACT OPTION B: 10-MONTH PREFERRED SERVICE AGREEMENT

* **Target Account:** Medium-to-large corporate offices, medical clinics, daycares, and facilities requiring high-frequency service (3x to 5x weekly).
* **Term:** Fixed ten (10) calendar months. *(Why 10 months? Aligns perfectly with Montreal corporate fiscal cycles and educational/commercial tenancies, capturing full seasonal shifts without the psychological barrier of an intimidating 12-month lock-in).*
* **Real Customer Economic Benefits:**
  1. **Preferred Billing Rate:** A 6% discount off standard rates (e.g., saving $168/month on a $2,800 account = **$1,680 total annual cash savings**).
  2. **Guaranteed Price Lock:** Immunity from statutory minimum wage increases (e.g., absorbing the November 2026 CPEEP decree wage adjustment).
  3. **Included Industrial Floor Maintenance:** One (1) complete machine floor scrubbing visit utilizing OTIS's **Tennant T300 automatic floor scrubber** during Month 5 ($450 standalone value, provided at no extra charge).
  4. **Priority Scheduling:** Guaranteed preferred night-shift time slots and priority emergency response.
* **Renewal:** Renews automatically for an additional 10-month term at the locked preferred rate upon 60 days' written confirmation.

## 7. FAIR & DEFENSIBLE EARLY TERMINATION ARCHITECTURE

In strict compliance with Articles 2125, 2129, and 1623 CCQ, OTIS's early termination structure balances commercial commitment with legal enforceability:

### Legitimate Termination Reasons (No Fee / Zero Penalty)
A customer may terminate the agreement immediately without any termination fee in the event of:
1. **Material Uncured Service Default:** OTIS fails to cure a documented service deficiency within forty-eight (48) hours of written notice pursuant to the Quality Remediation Protocol.
2. **Insolvency or Force Majeure:** The client permanently ceases operations, declares bankruptcy, or is forced to evacuate due to building condemnation.

### Early Termination Without Cause (Customer Convenience)
If a commercial client terminates a fixed-term agreement for convenience prior to the expiration date, the client is subject to a **commercially reasonable Early Termination Fee (ETF)** comprising:
1. **Notice Period Fulfillment:** Thirty (30) days' written notice for 6-month contracts; sixty (60) days' written notice for 10-month contracts. Service and standard billing continue during the notice period.
2. **Clawback of Promotional Discounts (Option B only):** Reimbursement of the actual monthly discount enjoyed to date (e.g., $150/mo discount over 4 months = $600 clawback).
3. **Clawback of Free Specialty Services Received:** If the client received the included Tennant floor machine scrub prior to termination, the client reimburses 50% of the standard standalone value ($225).
4. **Documented Onboarding Cost Recovery:** A fixed administrative onboarding fee of $250 to recover dedicated key management, security vetting, and site audit preparation.

> [!TIP]
> **Why Quebec Courts Enforce This:** This fee structure is 100% defensible under Art. 2129 CCQ because it recovers **actual unamortized discounts and real transition costs**. It does not attempt to claim unperformed future labor.

## 8. QUALITY PROTECTION & REMEDIATION PROTOCOL

To ensure contracts are commercially fair, OTIS incorporates an explicit, rapid-action **Service Remediation Protocol**:

```
Step 1: Formal Notification
• Client identifies a service deficiency and submits a written notice (email/text) within 24 hours of clean.
• Notice details specific location, missed task, and photographic evidence.
   ↓
Step 2: Rapid Acknowledgement (< 2 Hours)
• OTIS Operations Supervisor acknowledges receipt within 2 hours.
   ↓
Step 3: The 24-Hour Cure Window
• OTIS dispatches a supervisor or cleaning crew to re-clean and rectify the deficiency within 24 hours at ZERO cost to the client.
   ↓
Step 4: Supervisory Sign-Off
• Digital sign-off report sent to the facility manager.
   ↓
Step 5: Escalation for Chronic Default
• If OTIS incurs more than three (3) verified, uncured material service failures within a 60-day period, the client may terminate the agreement immediately with ZERO penalty.
```
