## 16. FRENCH / QUEBEC STRATEGY (BILL 96 & OQLF)

Under Quebec's **Bill 96** (Charte de la langue française):
1. Websites offering goods or services to Quebec consumers and businesses must be available in French in terms at least as favorable as any other language.
2. Invoices, contracts, employment documentation, and marketing collateral must be available in French.
3. Natural Quebec cleaning terminology must be used (e.g., *entretien ménager commercial*, *décapage et cirage de planchers*, *grand ménage après rénovation* instead of European terms like *pressing* or *nettoyage de locaux*).
