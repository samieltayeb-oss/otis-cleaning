# OTIS Commercial Cleaning: Paid Media Blueprint

## 1. Google Ads Strategy (Search Intent)
Google Ads is the primary acquisition channel for high-intent, bottom-of-funnel B2B leads. Since Zan has already proven this channel works, the goal is to scale efficiently.

### Keyword CPC Benchmarks (Montreal Market)
Competition in major cities like Montreal drives up CPCs for commercial services compared to residential cleaning. 
- **"commercial cleaning montreal"**: $10 - $20 / click
- **"office cleaning services"**: $12 - $25+ / click
- **"janitorial services montreal"**: $15 - $25+ / click
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL)**: Expect to pay between $60 and $200 per lead. Given the high lifetime value (LTV) and recurring nature of commercial contracts, these acquisition costs yield a strong ROI.

### Daily Budget Recommendation
- **Starting Budget**: $50 - $75/day (Minimum $1,500/month)
- *Why*: To get enough clicks (at $15 average CPC, $75 buys ~5 clicks per day). You need sufficient data volume for Google's algorithm to learn and optimize effectively.

### Bidding Strategy Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Days 1-30)**: **Maximize Clicks** (with a CPC cap) or **Manual CPC**. This ensures you control costs while gathering initial conversion data and identifying negative keywords.
- **Phase 2 (Days 31+)**: **Target CPA (Cost Per Action)**. Once the account has 15-30 conversions in a month, switch to Target CPA to let Google's machine learning find users most likely to fill out a quote request or call, rather than just clicking.
- **Critical Negative Keywords:** "jobs", "residential", "hiring", "house", "cheap".

## 2. Social Media Plan (Facebook & Instagram)
B2B cleaning is fundamentally "boring," so direct-response prospecting on FB/IG is often inefficient. Instead, **Facebook and Instagram will be used exclusively for retargeting** the warm traffic generated by Google Ads. 

### The Strategy: Building Trust & Authority
When an office manager clicks a Google Ad but doesn't convert immediately, they are comparison shopping. Your retargeting ads must remove friction and prove reliability.

### High-Converting Ad Creatives
Use the following visual formats to stand out and build trust:

#### A. The "Visual Proof" Carousel (Before & After)
- **Visuals**: High-contrast, side-by-side images or sliders showing transformations. (e.g. Dull, scuffed VCT flooring vs. freshly stripped and waxed floors gleaming under office lights).
- **Copy**: "First impressions matter. See how OTIS transforms Montreal workspaces. Request your free site walkthrough today."

#### B. The "Pristine Niche" Video
- **Visuals**: Slow panning shots of highly sensitive environments you clean. (e.g. A pristine clinic waiting room, focusing on sanitized high-touch surfaces, orderly magazine racks, and gleaming reception desks).
- **Copy**: "Healthcare standards for every office. We don't just clean; we sanitize to protect your staff and patients. See our compliance checklist."

#### C. Behind-the-Scenes Operations (Time-Lapse)
- **Visuals**: A 15-second time-lapse of the OTIS team in branded uniforms methodically cleaning a large open-plan office after hours. Focus on professional-grade equipment (backpack vacuums, floor scrubbers).
- **Copy**: "While Montreal sleeps, OTIS works. Consistent, audited cleaning processes so you walk into a perfect office every morning."

#### D. The Frictionless Offer (Lead Gen)
- **Visuals**: A clean, graphic image of a facility manager holding a clipboard or looking relieved, with a bold overlay: "Get Your Custom Quote in 24 Hours."
- **Copy**: "Still looking for the right cleaning partner? Let's schedule a 5-minute site visit. No pressure, just a transparent quote."

### Audience Segmentation
- **All Website Visitors (30 Days)**: Serve the visual proof and behind-the-scenes videos to build brand authority.
- **"Request a Quote" Abandoners**: If they hit the contact page but didn't submit, hit them with the "Frictionless Offer" ad featuring a strong Call to Action (CTA) like "Schedule a Site Walkthrough."
- **Industry-Specific Visitors**: If they visited a "Medical Office Cleaning" page, show them the Pristine Clinic video.
