# OTIS Operating System - ChatGPT Handover Document
**Context:** This document provides ChatGPT with the entire business logic, go-to-market strategy, and operational flow of the OTIS "Company in a Box." Please review the strategy, agent workflows, and assumptions, and advise on any business or operational blind spots.

## 1. The Core Philosophy
OTIS Commercial Cleaning is a tech-enabled B2B maintenance firm in Montreal. It competes on automation, legal compliance, and operational scale. 
* **The Math:** All pricing is anchored to the Quebec CPEEP Parity Decree ($23.00/h minimum wage). Bids target a $0.15 - $0.30/sqft rate to maintain a 40%+ gross margin.
* **The Staff:** The company operates with zero human office staff. 8 distinct AI agents execute all administrative, sales, and routing tasks. Zan (the CEO) acts only as the "Approver" via a Tri-State Command Console.

## 2. The B2B Go-To-Market Funnel
We do not rely on generic social media posts. We use the **Spear, Net, & Magnet** architecture:
1. **🎯 The Spear (LinkedIn Outbound):** Zan connects with Montreal Facility Managers, pitching authority content regarding Quebec labor laws, security liability, and health compliance.
2. **🕸️ The Net (Google Ads & SEO):** High-intent Search Campaigns ($15-$25 CPC) capture office managers actively searching for "reliable commercial cleaners" after their current vendor fails them.
3. **🧲 The Magnet (Meta Retargeting):** For 30 days, we pixel-track website visitors and retarget them on Facebook/Instagram with our High-End Cinematic Ad Posters to build omnipresent trust.

## 3. The 8-Agent Workflow (The Machine)
1. **Hunter (Sourcing):** Scrapes Google Maps API for specific B2B targets. Validates against CASL.
2. **Alex (Routing):** Intercepts Hunter's targets and calculates drive-time density. Rejects any lead further than a 15-minute drive from existing client clusters.
3. **Valérie (Sales/Proposals):** Drafts hyper-localized bilingual cold emails and calculates $0.15/sqft bidding math.
4. **Clara (Inbound/Inbox):** Operates the live website chat widget and monitors the `info@otiscc.ca` internal corporate inbox. Drafts replies to inbound leads automatically.
5. **Marcus (CapEx):** Analyzes square footage and amortizes heavy equipment rental costs into bids.
6. **Émile (Legal):** Audits outgoing contracts for Bill 96 compliance and injects the "Article 14 Joint Liability Shield."
7. **Bruno (Dispatch):** Dispatches human cleaners to physical locations via a free Telegram Bot, verifies GPS clock-ins, and releases security lockbox codes.
8. **Sophie (QA & Retention):** Monitors the 50-point QA checklist submitted by cleaners via the mobile Field App. Automatically generates a "Supply Addendum" upsell at Day 45 (20% margin on toilet paper/soap).
