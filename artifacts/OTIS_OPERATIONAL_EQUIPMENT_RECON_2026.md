## 5. Services Requiring Subcontractor / Partner (Out of Scope)

Under no circumstances should OTIS claim or attempt to self-perform the following without external certified partners:

1. **Exterior High-Rise Window Cleaning:**
   * Requires IRATA/SPRAT rope access technicians, boom lift rentals, city sidewalk permits, and specialized $5M–$10M liability riders.
   * *Action:* Partner with a certified Montreal window contractor; take a **15% to 20% referral broker fee**.
2. **Commercial Kitchen Exhaust Hood Degreasing (NFPA 96):**
   * Requires certified pressure-steam chemical rigs and municipal fire marshal compliance certificates.
3. **Specialized Mold, Asbestos, & Biohazard Remediation:**
   * Requires IICRC certifications, negative air scrubbers, full hazmat suits, and toxic waste disposal manifests.
