## 3. The 30-Second Free REQ Legal Owner Lookup

Once you find a high-potential business on Google Maps:
1. Open the **Registre des entreprises du Québec (REQ)**:  
   👉 [https://www.quebec.ca/entreprises-et-travailleurs-autonomes/registre-des-entreprises/rechercher-une-entreprise-au-registre](https://www.quebec.ca/entreprises-et-travailleurs-autonomes/registre-des-entreprises/rechercher-une-entreprise-au-registre)
2. In the search box, paste the exact business name (e.g. *Clinique Dentaire Notre-Dame* or *Studio Framestore*).
3. Click **Rechercher**. In the results, click the official file to view:
   * **Nom et prénom des administrateurs / dirigeants** (President, Vice-President, Secretary).
   * **Numéro d'entreprise du Québec (NEQ)**.
   * **Date d'immatriculation** (proves how established they are).
4. **Result:** You now possess the **full legal name of the owner/decision-maker**, their physical office address, and direct phone number. **Total cost: \$0.00.**

## 4. Quebec Law 25 & CASL B2B Compliance Rules

To maintain compliance and avoid fines:
* **Telephone Outreach:** Calling the publicly listed business telephone number on Google Maps during normal business hours to discuss commercial facility maintenance is **100% compliant under Canadian law**.
* **In-Person Walk-ins:** Dropping in to request a 10-minute floor audit does not violate anti-spam laws.
* **Electronic Messaging (Email/LinkedIn):** Under CASL Section 66(1)(b), you may only send a B2B cold email if the address was conspicuously published on their website or Google listing without a statement that the person does not wish to receive unsolicited commercial messages, **and** the message must be directly relevant to their business role.
