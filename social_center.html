Advanced Social Media Campaigns

Posters create brand awareness, but campaigns drive leads. Here are 3 highly-targeted content pillars to deploy across Montreal.

LinkedIn B2B Strategy

The "Founder's Desk" Series

Facility managers and CEOs are on LinkedIn. Instead of selling cleaning, Zan sells compliance and risk management. Position OTIS as the authority on Quebec labor laws.

Post: Break down the CPEEP /hr parity decree and explain "Joint Liability" to office managers.
Post: The true cost of "cheap" night cleaners (security breaches, unlocked doors, insurance voids).
Format: Text-only posts with 1 striking statistic, driving traffic to the OTIS Compliance PDF.
IG Reels & TikTok

The 60-Second Transformation

Cleaning is highly visual. Short-form video satisfies the psychological "itch" of watching something dirty become perfectly clean.

Video 1: Floor Stripping. Fast-motion video of a Tennant scrubber removing black scuff marks.
Video 2: Post-Construction. Time-lapse of a dusty office turning into a gleaming workspace.
Hook: "Watch us restore this Montreal office in 60 seconds..." with trending background audio.
