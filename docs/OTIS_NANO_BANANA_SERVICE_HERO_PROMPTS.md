## MASTER PRODUCTION NOTES (REVISED STANDARD)

### Aesthetic & Quality
- **Style:** Documentary commercial photography, editorial brand photography, or real-estate photography.
- **Lighting:** Natural practical lighting. Restrained color grading. Avoid excessive "cinematic" blue/orange contrasting unless it occurs naturally in the environment.
- **Realism:** Show real material imperfections. Believable Canadian interiors. Natural posture for workers. Physically correct cleaning techniques.
- **Avoid:** No "8k/ultra-realistic/mirror finish" buzzwords. Avoid plastic skin, perfect symmetry, or fantasy architecture. NO FAKE TEXT or LOGOS in the images.

### Aspect Ratio & Resolution
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (Landscape)
- **Target Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Minimum)

### Composition Rules (STRICT)
- **Left-Third Rule:** The left 33% of the image MUST be relatively clear, dark, and free of highly distracting focal points or faces. This is the "safe zone" for the H1 typography and primary CTA buttons. 
- **CSS Overlay:** The production UI will apply `linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(8,13,26,0.88) 0%, rgba(8,13,26,0.45) 45%, rgba(8,13,26,0) 100%)`.
- **Variety:** Do not force a cleaner into every hero. Use the four approved composition families below.
