## PREAMBLE
WHEREAS the Contractor is an established commercial facility maintenance and cleaning company operating in Greater Montreal, possessing certified personnel, specialized equipment, and comprehensive liability insurance;  
WHEREAS the Client desires to retain the services of the Contractor to perform recurring commercial cleaning services at its designated business premises;  
WHEREAS the Parties acknowledge that this is a **Business-to-Business (B2B) commercial contract** between sophisticated commercial entities, and the provisions of the *Consumer Protection Act* (Quebec) do not apply to this transaction;  

NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION OF THE MUTUAL COVENANTS CONTAINED HEREIN, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

***

### 1. TERM OF AGREEMENT
1.1. **Fixed Term:** This Agreement is entered into for a firm period of **six (6) consecutive calendar months** (the **"Initial Term"**).  
1.2. **Commencement Date:** Service shall commence on **[Start Date, e.g., October 1, 2026]** (the **"Start Date"**).  
1.3. **Expiration Date:** Unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms herein, this Agreement shall conclude on **[End Date, e.g., March 31, 2027]** (the **"End Date"**).  

### 2. SERVICE PREMISES & FREQUENCY
2.1. **Premises Location:** The cleaning services shall be rendered exclusively at the following commercial facility:  
   * **Facility Name:** [Client Facility Name]  
   * **Address:** [Street Address, Suite/Floor, City, Postal Code]  
   * **Designated Area:** Approximately [Square Footage, e.g., 4,500] rentable square feet.  
2.2. **Service Frequency:** Services shall be performed **[Frequency, e.g., 3 times per week / 5 times per week]**, on the following designated days: **[e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday]**.  
2.3. **Service Hours:** Services shall be performed after regular business hours, between the hours of **[e.g., 6:00 PM and 11:00 PM]**, unless otherwise agreed in writing.  

### 3. SCOPE OF WORK (SOW) & TASKS
3.1. **Standard Services:** The Contractor shall perform the tasks itemized in **Schedule "A" (Scope of Work)**, attached hereto and forming an integral part of this Agreement, encompassing:  
   * *General Areas & Corridors:* Emptying wastebaskets, replacing liners, dusting desks/horizontal surfaces, vacuuming carpeted areas with HEPA filtration units, damp-mopping hard floors.  
   * *Restrooms:* Sanitizing and descaling toilets, urinals, sinks, and countertops with hospital-grade disinfectant; polishing mirrors and chrome fixtures; mopping floors with disinfectant solution; restocking soap, paper towel, and toilet paper dispensers.  
   * *Kitchenettes & Breakrooms:* Cleaning and wiping tables, countertops, and exterior appliance surfaces; sanitizing sinks; spot-cleaning cabinet fronts; damp-mopping flooring.  
3.2. **Exclusions from Standard Scope:** Unless specifically contracted as an Additional Service under Section 11, the Scope of Work strictly excludes:  
   * Exterior high-rise window cleaning;  
   * Hazardous waste handling, bio-hazardous spills, needle disposal, or chemical remediation;  
   * Moving heavy furniture, server racks, or filing cabinets exceeding 25 kg;  
   * Machine floor stripping, waxing, or crystallization (available as separate scheduled specialty services);  
   * Washing dishes or organizing interior personal filing cabinets.  

### 4. PRICING & PAYMENT TERMS
4.1. **Monthly Service Fee:** In consideration of the services rendered, the Client shall pay to the Contractor a fixed monthly service fee of:  
   * **CAD $[Amount, e.g., 1,850.00]** per calendar month (the **"Monthly Fee"**).  
4.2. **Applicable Taxes:** The Monthly Fee is exclusive of applicable federal and provincial sales taxes. The Client shall pay the Goods and Services Tax (GST - 5%) and Quebec Sales Tax (QST - 9.975%) on all invoiced amounts.  
4.3. **Invoicing & Due Date:** Invoices shall be issued on the first (1st) day of each calendar month for that month's service. Invoices are payable **Net 15 days** from the invoice date (the **"Due Date"**).  
4.4. **Method of Payment:** Payment shall be made via Pre-Authorized Debit (PAD), Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT / Interac Direct), or corporate credit card.  
4.5. **Late Payment Charges:** Any past-due amount not received within thirty (30) days of the invoice date shall accrue interest at the rate of **1.5% per month (18% per annum)**, calculated daily from the Due Date until paid in full. The Client shall also reimburse the Contractor for all reasonable legal and collection fees incurred to recover delinquent sums.  

### 5. SUPPLIES, CHEMICALS & EQUIPMENT
5.1. **Contractor Equipment & Supplies:** OTIS shall provide all cleaning machinery, professional vacuums, microfiber cleaning cloths, dual-bucket mop systems, and non-toxic, neutral pH cleaning solutions certified under UL EcoLogo or equivalent standards.  
5.2. **Consumable Restroom Paper & Soap:** Unless explicitly included in Schedule "A", consumable paper products (toilet tissue, paper hand towels) and hand soaps shall be supplied by the Client. OTIS can procure and replenish these consumables on behalf of the Client at cost plus a 15% procurement and stocking fee.  

### 6. FACILITY ACCESS & SECURITY PROTOCOLS
6.1. **Access Provision:** The Client shall provide the Contractor with necessary building access keys, electronic fobs, and security alarm arm/disarm codes prior to the Commencement Date.  
6.2. **Key Security:** OTIS shall log all keys and fobs in a secure, encrypted key management system. Keys shall be held exclusively by assigned, vetted cleaning technicians and supervisory personnel.  
6.3. **Premises Security:** The Contractor's personnel shall ensure that all designated doors and windows are secured and that facility alarms are armed upon completion of each service shift, in accordance with the Client's written security instructions.  

### 7. QUALITY ASSURANCE & REMEDIATION PROTOCOL
7.1. **Performance Standard:** OTIS shall perform all services in a professional, workmanlike manner, in accordance with industry standards established by ISSA Canada and in compliance with Quebec workplace safety standards.  
7.2. **Deficiency Reporting:** If the Client determines that any portion of the Scope of Work was not performed or was performed deficiently, the Client shall provide written notice to OTIS via email at `operations@otiscc.ca` within twenty-four (24) hours following the clean.  
7.3. **The 24-Hour Cure Obligation:** Upon receipt of written deficiency notice, OTIS shall acknowledge receipt within two (2) hours during business hours, and shall dispatch supervisory or cleaning staff to **re-clean and remediate the deficient area within twenty-four (24) hours at zero additional cost to the Client**.  
7.4. **Initial 30-Day Performance Review:** During the first thirty (30) days following the Start Date, if the Contractor materially fails to meet the agreed Scope of Work and fails to remediate repeated written deficiencies within the 24-hour cure window, the Client may cancel this Agreement at Day 30 without any Early Termination Fee by providing written notice.  

### 8. CONTRACTOR REPRESENTATIONS & INSURANCE
8.1. **Insurance Coverage:** The Contractor maintains, and shall maintain throughout the Term, a Commercial General Liability (CGL) insurance policy with a coverage limit of not less than **CAD $5,000,000 per occurrence**, covering property damage and bodily injury. Proof of insurance (Certificate of Insurance) naming the Client as an additional insured shall be provided upon request.  
8.2. **CNESST Compliance:** The Contractor is registered in good standing with the *Commission des normes, de l'équité, de la santé et de la sécurité du travail* (CNESST) and shall provide a current letter of compliance upon request.  
8.3. **Quebec Decree Compliance:** The Contractor complies strictly with the provisions of the *Decree respecting the cleaning of public buildings in Greater Montreal* (CPEEP), guaranteeing that all personnel are paid at or above mandatory statutory wage floors (23/hr base minimum as of March 2026), fully shielding the Client from joint liability under Article 14 of the *Act respecting collective agreement decrees*.  

### 9. DAMAGE & INCIDENT REPORTING
9.1. **Incident Protocol:** The Contractor shall report any accidental property damage or pre-existing facility damage to the Client within twelve (12) hours of occurrence.  
9.2. **Liability Limitation:** The Contractor's liability for direct physical damage to the Client's property resulting from the Contractor's gross negligence or willful misconduct shall be covered by the Contractor's CGL policy. The Contractor shall not be liable for indirect, consequential, or economic loss, nor for damage arising from pre-existing structural decay or improperly mounted wall fixtures.  

### 10. CONFIDENTIALITY
10.1. **Confidential Information:** The Contractor and its personnel shall hold in strict confidence all proprietary business information, client documents, intellectual property, or confidential corporate materials visible or accessible within the Client's facility during the performance of services. Technicians are prohibited from reading, copying, or photographing any customer documentation.  

### 11. CHANGE OF SCOPE & ADDITIONAL SERVICES
11.1. **Facility Expansion:** If the Client expands its physical premises, requests additional cleaning days, or increases facility square footage, the Monthly Fee shall be adjusted proportionally by mutual written amendment.  
11.2. **Specialty Work Orders:** Additional one-time services (e.g., machine floor scrubbing, post-construction cleanup, window washing) requested by the Client shall be invoiced separately pursuant to a written work authorization at OTIS's preferred client rates.  

### 12. RENEWAL & EXPIRATION
12.1. **End-of-Term Transition:** At least thirty (30) days prior to the Expiration Date, the Parties shall confer regarding contract renewal.  
12.2. **Month-to-Month Conversion:** If neither Party provides written notice of non-renewal or submits a new term agreement prior to the Expiration Date, this Agreement shall automatically convert to a **month-to-month service agreement** at the standard prevailing rate, terminable by either Party upon thirty (30) days' advance written notice.  

### 13. TERMINATION & RESILIATION
13.1. **Modification of Article 2125 CCQ:** The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that, in consideration of the Contractor's allocation of dedicated equipment, personnel, and fixed scheduling, the Client's right of unilateral resiliation under Article 2125 of the *Civil Code of Québec* is hereby **expressly modified and circumscribed by the provisions of this Section 13**.  
13.2. **Termination for Cause (Default):** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party:  
   * Files for bankruptcy, enters liquidation, or ceases commercial operations;  
   * Fails to cure a material breach of this Agreement (including non-payment by the Client, or repeated uncured service failures by OTIS under Section 7) within seven (7) business days following receipt of formal written default notice.  
13.3. **Termination Without Cause (Client Convenience):** If the Client elects to resiliate or terminate this Agreement prior to the End Date for reasons other than an uncured material default by OTIS, the Client shall provide **thirty (30) days' advance written notice**, continue payment for services rendered during said notice period, and pay to OTIS an **Early Termination Transition Fee of CAD $250.00** representing documented administrative onboarding and re-staffing costs, pursuant to Article 2129 CCQ.  

### 14. FORCE MAJEURE
14.1. Neither Party shall be held liable for failure or delay in performing its obligations where such failure arises from an unforeseeable, irresistible event beyond its reasonable control (*force majeure* under Article 1470 CCQ), including natural disasters, governmental building closures, or severe structural fire.  

### 15. DISPUTE RESOLUTION & JURISDICTION
15.1. **Good Faith Negotiation:** In the event of any disagreement arising from this Agreement, executive representatives of both Parties shall meet within five (5) business days to resolve the matter amicably.  
15.2. **Applicable Law:** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **Province of Quebec** and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.  
15.3. **Judicial District:** The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent Courts of the **Judicial District of Montreal**, Province of Quebec.
