## 5. Fatal Blindspots & Critical Adjustments

### 1. The Daycare & CPE Language Trap (Bill 96 / OQLF)
* **Zan's Assumption**: Stick strictly to English for all 37 leads during this sprint.
* **The Danger**: 6 of the leads are **Centres de la petite enfance (CPEs)** or daycares operating under Ministère de la Famille regulations. In Quebec, public and subsidized childcare directors operate formally in French. Sending unrequested English marketing material can trigger an OQLF complaint and guarantees an immediate rejection.
* **The Fix**: Create a clean French version of the 1-page pre-winter floor care email and script (*"Entretien préhivernal des planchers"*) specifically for Francophone daycares and managers.

### 2. Resolving the Post-Reno Décret Question (CPEEP vs CCQ)
* **Zan's Note**: *"⚠️ Open: whether post-reno work inside an édifice public is décret-covered. Worth roughly $7/hr on the cost basis. One call: CPEEP, 514 384-6640."*
* **The Legal Answer**:
  * If cleaning occurs **while construction trades are still on site and before the general contractor achieves substantial completion**, it is classified as construction work under the **CCQ (Commission de la construction du Québec)**. Wages are $35–$45/hr.
  * If cleaning occurs **after the general contractor hands over the space to the tenant/owner for occupancy**, it falls under the **CPEEP Décret** at the standard commercial cleaner rate (**23/hr**).
  * **Rule for OTIS**: Only bid on post-construction cleaning **after final trade completion (handover phase)**, explicitly stating in the contract: *"Entretien de finition post-rénovation après livraison de chantier."*

### 3. Rate Card Anchoring
* **Zan's Point**: The 6¢/sq ft floor published on older marketing materials is dangerously low.
* **The Correction**: Commercial floor stripping and waxing in Greater Montreal benchmarks at **$0.35 to $0.65 per square foot** (depending on coats of wax and wax buildup), with a **minimum charge of $450–$650**. Deep cleaning benchmarks at **$0.20 to $0.35 per square foot**. Never quote under 25¢ for floor restoration.
