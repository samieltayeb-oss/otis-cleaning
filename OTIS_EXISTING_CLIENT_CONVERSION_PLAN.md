## 1. STRATEGIC POSITIONING & CORE CONVERSION PHILOSOPHY

> **THE CARDINAL RULE: NEVER ISSUE AN ULTIMATUM.**  
> We do NOT send an email saying: *"We are changing our policy. You must sign this contract or we will stop cleaning your facility."*

### The Conversion Frame: "The VIP Service Certainty & Rate Lock"
The conversation is framed entirely around **protecting the customer**:
1. **Quebec Regulatory Context (The Price Lock):** The Quebec CPEEP Parity Decree mandates cleaning wage hikes in late 2026. Commercial cleaning rates across Montreal are increasing. We are reaching out to our core legacy clients to offer a **guaranteed rate freeze**.
2. **Staffing & Schedule Certainty:** In post-pandemic Montreal, facility managers fear staff turnover and missed cleans. A formal agreement guarantees a dedicated, primary cleaning crew and locked night-shift hours.
3. **Formal Quality Commitments (The 24-Hour Cure Shield):** Introducing our new formalized SLA—including supervisory audits, the 24-Hour Re-Clean Guarantee, and emergency response.
4. **Tangible Loyalty Upgrade:** Adding an industrial machine floor care visit using our **Tennant T300 automatic floor scrubber** at zero cost.
