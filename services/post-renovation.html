Post-Construction Turnover Scope

Every shift follows our structured quality protocol logged into our encrypted operational ledger.

01

Multi-Stage Airborne Dust Extraction

Vacuuming and wiping down ceiling fixtures, vents, light tracks, and upper ledges with microfibers.

02

Drywall & Plaster Residue Removal

Detailed wiping of walls, door jams, trim, and switches to remove fine white drywall powder.

03

Adhesive, Paint & Silicone Cleanup

Gentle razor-blade and solvent removal of tape residue, paint spatters, and silicone from glass and tiles.

04

Cabinet Interior & Millwork Detailing

Vacuuming inside drawers, kitchen cupboards, utility closets, and wiping shelf surfaces.

05

Final Floor Wash & Machine Scrub

Thorough multi-pass hard floor washing to eliminate construction dust haze before tenant walkthrough.
