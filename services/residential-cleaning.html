Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our scheduling, protocols, insurance, and equipment.

Do you send the same cleaning team every time?

Yes! For recurring weekly or bi-weekly residential clients, we assign the same dedicated team so they become familiar with your home's preferences and requirements.

Are your cleaning professionals insured and bonded?

Absolutely. Every OTIS cleaner is covered by our $2,000,000 commercial liability policy and CNESST, with strict background and identity verification.

What cleaning products do you use?

We utilize professional-grade, neutral-pH commercial products that are non-toxic, eco-certified, and safe for delicate hardwood finishes, marble, and pets.
