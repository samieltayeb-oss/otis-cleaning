Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our scheduling, protocols, insurance, and equipment.

What is the difference between standard cleaning and deep cleaning?

Standard cleaning maintains regular hygiene (surfaces, floors, trash). Deep cleaning targets neglected areas: behind appliances, scrubbing tile grout, washing baseboards by hand, and removing accumulated lime and grease.

How often should I book a deep clean?

Most clients schedule a deep clean twice a year (Spring and Fall), or before major events, seasonal tenant turnovers, and after renovations.

Do I need to supply cleaning products or equipment?

No. OTIS brings professional-grade equipment, commercial HEPA vacuums, specialized microfiber sets, and eco-certified neutral cleaning solutions.

How long does a full deep cleaning take?

Depending on the square footage and current condition, a residential or commercial deep clean typically takes between 4 and 8 hours with a multi-person crew.
