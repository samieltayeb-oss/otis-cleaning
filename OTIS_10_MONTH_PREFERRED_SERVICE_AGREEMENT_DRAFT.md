## PREAMBLE
WHEREAS the Contractor is an established commercial facility maintenance company in Greater Montreal, operating an industrial machine fleet and maintaining certified compliance under the Quebec CPEEP Decree;  
WHEREAS the Client desires to retain the Contractor under a preferred, high-retention commercial service relationship to maintain superior hygienic and aesthetic standards across its premises;  
WHEREAS in consideration of the Client's ten (10) month operating commitment, the Contractor has agreed to provide **preferred monthly pricing, a guaranteed price lock, dedicated priority scheduling, and complimentary industrial machine floor scrubbing**;  
WHEREAS the Parties expressly acknowledge that this is a **Business-to-Business (B2B) commercial contract** between sophisticated commercial legal persons;  

NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION OF THE MUTUAL COVENANTS CONTAINED HEREIN, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

In consideration of the 10-Month term commitment, OTIS grants the Client the following concrete operational and financial benefits:

2.3. **Included Industrial Floor Maintenance Visit:** During Month 5 or Month 6 of the Term, OTIS shall perform **one (1) comprehensive machine floor scrubbing and deep conditioning visit** across all primary corridor and lobby hard floors using OTIS's commercial **Tennant T300 automatic walk-behind floor scrubber** (a **CAD $450.00 standalone commercial value, provided at zero additional charge**).  
2.4. **Specialty Work Order Discount:** The Client shall receive a **15% discount** on all additional periodic specialty work orders (e.g., carpet extraction, post-event cleanups, internal partition glass washing) ordered during the Term.  
2.5. **Priority Scheduling:** Dedicated assignment of a consistent primary cleaning crew and guaranteed **2-hour operational response** for urgent facility dispatch.

### 7. FACILITY ACCESS & SECURITY PROTOCOLS
7.1. The Client shall supply necessary keys, electronic keycards, and security alarm arming codes.  
7.2. Keys are logged in OTIS's secure key management vault. Facilities are secured, locked, and armed upon departure.  

### 8. QUALITY ASSURANCE & SERVICE REMEDIATION PROTOCOL
8.1. **The Performance Guarantee:** OTIS warrants that all services shall meet or exceed professional ISSA Canada commercial standards.  
8.2. **Formal Deficiency Notice:** If the Client observes any service deficiency, notice shall be transmitted in writing within twenty-four (24) hours to `operations@otiscc.ca`.  
8.3. **The 24-Hour Cure Obligation:** OTIS shall acknowledge receipt within two (2) hours, and shall dispatch staff to **re-clean and remediate the reported area within twenty-four (24) hours at zero expense to the Client**.  
8.4. **Chronic Uncured Default Protection:** If OTIS incurs more than three (3) verified, uncured material service deficiencies within any sixty (60) day period, the Client shall have the right to **terminate this Agreement immediately with zero early termination fee**, pursuant to Section 13.2.  

### 9. CONTRACTOR CERTIFICATIONS & INSURANCE
9.1. **Insurance:** OTIS maintains Commercial General Liability insurance of **CAD $5,000,000 per occurrence**. Certificate of Insurance naming the Client as additional insured provided upon request.  
9.2. **CNESST Compliance:** OTIS guarantees active registration and full compliance with the *Commission des normes, de l'équité, de la santé et de la sécurité du travail* (CNESST).  
9.3. **Quebec Decree Compliance:** OTIS complies with the *Decree respecting the cleaning of public buildings in Greater Montreal* (CPEEP), guaranteeing that all workers receive statutory minimum wages (23/hr floor), fully protecting the Client under Article 14 of the *Act respecting collective agreement decrees*.  

### 10. DAMAGE REPORTING & CONFIDENTIALITY
10.1. Any accidental damage shall be reported within twelve (12) hours.  
10.2. Technicians are bound by strict confidentiality; reading or handling client business files is strictly prohibited.  

### 11. CHANGE OF SCOPE
11.1. Any expansion of square footage or alteration of service frequency shall be formalized by written addendum adjusting the Monthly Fee proportionally.  

### 12. RENEWAL MECHANISM
12.1. Sixty (60) days prior to the End Date, OTIS shall submit renewal documentation.  
12.2. If the Client elects to renew for an additional 10-month term, the preferred rate and annual complimentary Tennant machine scrub shall continue.  
12.3. Absent written non-renewal, the contract converts to month-to-month service at the standard rate.  

### 13. TERMINATION, RESILIATION & DEFENSIBLE FEE STRUCTURE
13.1. **Circumscription of Article 2125 CCQ:** The Parties expressly agree that, in light of the significant rate discounts, guaranteed price lock, dedicated equipment allocation, and included complimentary machine floor services granted to the Client, the Client's right of unilateral resiliation under Article 2125 of the *Civil Code of Québec* is hereby **expressly modified, circumscribed, and governed by this Section 13**.  
13.2. **Termination for Cause (Zero Fee):** The Client may terminate this Agreement immediately without fee or penalty if:  
   * OTIS commits an uncured material default pursuant to Section 8.4;  
   * The Client permanently ceases business operations due to bankruptcy or building condemnation.  
13.3. **Termination Without Cause (Client Convenience):** If the Client resiliates or terminates this Agreement prior to the End Date for reasons other than an uncured material default by OTIS, the Client shall:  
   a) Provide **sixty (60) days' advance written notice** and pay for services rendered through the notice period;  
   b) **Clawback of Realized Preferred Rate Discounts:** Reimburse the Contractor for the actual promotional rate discount enjoyed by the Client from the Start Date through the effective termination date (calculated as the difference between the standard commercial rate and the preferred rate, capped at $150.00/month);  
   c) **Specialty Service Recovery:** If the complimentary Tennant machine floor scrub was performed prior to the early termination date, reimburse **fifty percent (50%) of the standalone value (CAD $225.00)**;  
   d) **Onboarding Administration Recovery:** Pay a flat fee of **CAD $250.00** representing unrecovered facility key management, site auditing, and staff vetting costs.  
13.4. **Statutory Enforceability Acknowledgement:** The Client expressly acknowledges that the early termination fees set forth in Section 13.3 constitute a **fair, reasonable pre-estimate of direct unamortized onboarding expenses, clawback of unearned promotional price concessions, and transition costs** pursuant to Article 2129 of the *Civil Code of Québec*, and do not constitute an abusive penalty under Article 1623 CCQ.
