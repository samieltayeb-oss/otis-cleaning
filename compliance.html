Quebec Statutory Parity

Regulatory Compliance & Risk Mitigation

Enterprise janitorial governance designed to protect building owners, property managers, and facility executives from legal and labor liability.

In Quebec's commercial property sector, contracting a janitorial provider that violates provincial labor decrees can expose building owners and prime tenants to joint statutory liability. OTIS operates with full transparency, ensuring complete compliance with the Comité paritaire de l'entretien d'édifices publics (CPEEP), CNESST health standards, and comprehensive commercial liability insurance.

Legal Note: Under Section 39 of the Quebec Act respecting collective agreement decrees, building owners and prime tenants may be held jointly and solidarily liable for unpaid decree wages if an unregulated cleaning contractor underbids statutory minimums.
