Clinical Sanitization Scope

Every shift follows our structured quality protocol logged into our encrypted operational ledger.

01

Waiting Room & Reception Disinfection

Sanitizing check-in counters, card terminals, waiting chairs, and entry touchpoints.

02

Operatory & Examination Room Care

Detailed wiping of exam tables, cabinetry, sink fixtures, and clinical lighting switches.

03
