PART 02   ·   HOW WE DO IT  ·  THE WAY IN
Nobody wins a cleaning contract cold
Most businesses already have a cleaner. Winning a contract cold means displacing them, which needs a trigger nobody can 
manufacture. So we go in sideways - through two doors that need no displacement at all.
ROUTE 1
The one-off job
Strip and wax, post-construction, deep clean, move-out. Additive work 
the current cleaner does not do, so nobody has to be sacked for Otis to 
be hired.
It is a paid trial in both directions. The buyer risks nothing, and Otis gets 
inside the building where the standard is watched rather than claimed.
ROUTE 2
The backup list
"Put us on your list for when your cleaner does not turn up." No 
budget, no decision, no displacement - the easiest yes in the industry.
When the current cleaner no-shows, Otis is the call. The comparison gets 
made for the buyer instead of argued at them.
A one-off job is never the end of the story. Every completed job opens the contract conversation - see Part Four.

DEADLINE-DRIVEN

3 Deep clean and high-dusting
SEASONAL

4 Commercial move-in / move-out
TRIGGERED
New tenancy. Commercial only.

market runs 15-30 cents per square foot; the published floor of 6 cents sits below it and anchors a buyer low.

Three regimes, and one of them sells for you
CASL
$1M / $10M PENALTIES
The burden of proof sits on the sender. No 
lead is emailed without a traceable source and 
a date. If the address cannot be traced to a 
page the business published, it is phone-only - 
19 of the 37 are.
Competition Act s.74.011
SENDER IDENTITY
A false or misleading sender name is 
reviewable conduct, and the defence that 
nobody was actually deceived has been 
removed. Mail goes from a real address under 
a real name.
CNESST and the decret
YOUR BEST ARGUMENT
The joint-liability rule makes the CLIENT 
responsible for an underpriced rival's unpaid 
wages. That is not a burden - it is the strongest 
line against a cheaper competitor, and it opens 
on their exposure, not on Otis.
Two working rules that follow from this: contact-data brokers are never a source, because they sell addresses the business never published - and a published address 
that is not the facilities buyer, like an admissions inbox, is a call rather than an email.
Every row in the tracker carries where the contact came from and the date it was collected, so the position holds up without anybody having to reconstruct it later.

PART 04   ·   WHERE IT GOES  ·  THE NEXT SIX WEEKS
Turning jobs into contracts, and nine reviews into twenty
EVERY COMPLETED JOB OPENS A CONTRACT
• The conversation is with somebody who has already watched the 
standard happen in their own building
• It is written into the outreach as a step, not left to whoever 
remembers - a job delivered and never followed by that conversation 
is a job done, not a client won
• The tracker records contract stage and monthly value, so the number 
is visible rather than hoped for
• Recurring work is retention and expansion. It is where this leads, not 
where it starts
THE REVIEW PUSH - NINE TOWARD TWENTY
• Every review is five stars already - the hard part is done. The gap is 
volume, not quality
• Ask every past and present client, systematically, and ask at 
completion every time from now on
• Not staff, friends or family: Google prohibits reviews from anyone 
with a conflict of interest, and removals are visible at this volume
• Never attach the referral discount to a review - an incentive in 
exchange for one is prohibited outright
• Steer the wording toward commercial: janitorial, contract, our 
building
