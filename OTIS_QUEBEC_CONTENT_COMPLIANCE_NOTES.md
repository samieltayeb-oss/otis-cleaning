## PART 1: BILL 96 / CHARTER OF THE FRENCH LANGUAGE

### Legal Status of OTIS Website (English-only)
**Classification: ACTIVE STATUTORY VIOLATION**

Under Section 52 of the *Charter of the French Language* (CQLR c C-11), commercial websites offering products or services in Quebec must be available in French. Operating an English-only commercial website in Montreal is an active violation. A full French `/fr/` version is **legally mandatory**, not optional.

### OTIS Francisation Certificate Requirements
- OTIS has fewer than 25 employees → **EXEMPT from formal OQLF francisation certification**
- This exemption does NOT reduce other obligations (website, advertising, contracts, customer service)

### Contract Requirements (§55)
**Contracts of Adhesion** (pre-drafted standard service agreements):
1. Must be drafted in French
2. French version must be delivered to client FIRST
3. Client may only elect English version AFTER receiving French version
4. An English-only contract is potentially null under §204.21

**Action Required:** Translate all 6-month and 10-month service agreements to French immediately.

### Public Signage (§58) — Since June 1, 2025
- French must have "marked predominance" (at least 2× visual area/size of English)
- Even unregistered trademarks like "OTIS" must be accompanied by a French generic descriptor (e.g., "Entretien commercial OTIS")
- Applies to: physical signage at 2447 Ave Madison, fleet vehicle decals, any outdoor displays

## PART 2: MARKETING CLAIMS — LEGAL RISK MATRIX

### Competition Act (Canada) + Quebec Consumer Protection

| Claim | Risk Level | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| "Hospital-grade cleaning" | 🟡 SAFE WITH MODIFICATION | Change to: "Health Canada-registered commercial disinfectants" |
| "Medical-grade disinfection" | 🔴 DO NOT USE | No statutory definition; treated as puffery |
| "ISSA certified" | 🔴 DO NOT USE | OTIS is a member, not certified (CIMS). Change to: "ISSA Canada Member #102438" |
| "Health authority approved" | 🔴 DO NOT USE | No such approval exists from any Canadian authority |
| "100% disinfected" | 🔴 DO NOT USE | Scientifically false; creates strict CCQ warranty liability |
| "Eliminates all germs" | 🔴 DO NOT USE | Absolute claim; no disinfectant eliminates 100% |
| "Non-toxic cleaning products" | 🔴 DO NOT USE (absolute) | Violates Bill C-59 greenwashing rules |
| "Eco-certified products" | 🟡 SAFE IF PROVEN | Only if UL EcoLogo products are actually purchased and used |
| "Child-safe products" | 🟡 RISKY — MODIFY | Use: "Fragrance-free, low-residue, suitable for educational environments" |
| "Guaranteed sterile environment" | 🔴 DO NOT USE | "Sterility" = zero microorganisms; impossible in commercial spaces |
| "Government-approved protocols" | 🔴 DO NOT USE | No government approval of private janitorial protocols exists |
| "ISO certified" | 🔴 DO NOT USE | OTIS has no ISO registrar certificate — would be outright fraud |
| "WHMIS certified" | 🔴 DO NOT USE (as badge) | WHMIS is a legal requirement, not a certification achievement |
| "Cleaning of clinical environments" | ✅ SAFE WITH SCOPE LIMITS | Routine cleaning of private clinics ok; exclude sterile surgical suites |
| "CNESST compliant" | ✅ SAFE | Legally required; frame as compliance standard not badge |
| "Fully insured and bonded" | 🟡 VERIFY COVERAGE | Owner must confirm current policy amounts before publishing |
| "UL Eco-Certified" (badge in footer) | 🟡 VERIFY | Owner must confirm actual product purchases |
| "Conforme CPEEP" | 🟡 VERIFY | Owner must confirm wage compliance — powerful trust signal if accurate |
